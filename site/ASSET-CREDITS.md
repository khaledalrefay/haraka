# Asset credits

## Original movement diagrams

All `assets/exercises/*.svg` except `shoulder-rolls-reference.svg` are original functional joint-and-segment movement diagrams authored for this project. They are provided under the project's MIT license. They are schematic educational cues, not anatomical measurements or clinically reviewed exercise prescriptions. No externally sourced illustration was traced.
